HụiKeeper 2.0

Improving a hụi-tracking app with SCAMPER

A big informal market

  • Hụi and bốc bát họ circles are everywhere
  • Black credit and pawnshops fill the gaps
  • Formal finance does not reach many households

High demand, little protection.

Where the gap is

The product today

  • Flutter app for Android and iOS
  • Fully offline: SQLite on the device
  • Dead hụi (no interest) and live hụi (interest)
  • Paid / unpaid, actual amount, notes
  • Stats: paid, remaining, progress

How it is built, and where changes land

One hụi cycle: everyone pays, one member collects

SCAMPER: seven questions for one product

SSubstituteWhat can be replaced or swapped?
CCombineWhat can be merged into one?
AAdaptWhat else is like this, and what can be copied from it?
MModifyWhat can be made bigger, smaller, faster, slower or different?
PPut to another useWho else could use it, and where else?
EEliminateWhat can be removed without losing the core value?
RRearrange / ReverseWhat if the order, roles or layout were reversed?

Fewer steps to record a payment

SSubstitute

One-tap “Paid” replaces typing amounts.

Recording a payment takes seconds instead of a form.
CCombine

Ledger, reminder and payment QR on one card.

Fewer screens between knowing a due date and settling it.

Borrow what works, then flex it

AAdapt

Loan-app due / overdue timeline; share receipts like a chat.

Users already understand these patterns, so there is nothing new to learn.
MModify

Flexible cycle length and a large-text mode.

One app fits many circle rhythms and many kinds of users.

Widen it, strip it, flip it

PPut to another use

Savings-goal tracker and payment history.

The same data model creates value beyond hụi.
EEliminate

No sign-up, no internet, auto-calculated payouts.

Less friction, less risk, and full privacy on the device.
RRearrange / Reverse

Player view first, host view one tap away.

The home screen answers the question users actually ask first.

The new version, on one screen

What changes, and what comes next

  • Fewer taps per payment
  • Reminders before every due date
  • Nothing to sign up for, still offline
