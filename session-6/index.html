Innovative Technology

Three case studies from Chapter 3: AI adoption in SMEs, Blockchain in enterprise and supply-chain management, and network platforms. Includes a worked Blockchain application example for the Health Insurance industry.

What's covered

1 · AI in SMEs

Three adoption paths (off-the-shelf, embedded, custom), strategic drivers vs. adoption barriers, and Vietnam's 93% SME AI-adoption rate (Deloitte/Meta 2025) against the OECD policy framework.

2 · Blockchain

Core concept (distributed, immutable ledger), the farm-to-table food traceability case, six characteristics of blockchain as a data platform, and a full worked example: a Blockchain application for Health Insurance.

3 · Network Platforms

How platforms create value for SMEs (network effects, reach, outsourcing), the three platform models, and the strategic risks of lock-in and data asymmetry.
